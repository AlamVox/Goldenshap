Bethuadahari, Nadia.
Where Tradition Lives.

In the heart of Nadia district, West Bengal, our master climbers (Shiuli) scale towering date palm trees before dawn when the winter chill is crispest. The earthen pots collect the pristine sap undisturbed overnight.

Within hours of daybreak, the fresh nectar is poured into massive iron cauldrons and slow-boiled over firewood. No hydros, no preservatives, and no white sugar fillers are ever added. What you taste is pure Bengal winter sweetness.
